# Complete List of Publicly Available Works of John Owen

## About John Owen (1616-1683)

John Owen was an English Puritan Nonconformist church leader, theologian, and vice-chancellor of the University of Oxford. He was one of the most prominent theologians in England during his lifetime and a prolific author who wrote articles, treatises, Biblical commentaries, poetry, children's catechisms, and other works. Owen penned nearly 100 volumes representing more than 7 million words during his life, issuing more than eighty separate publications.

## Additional Notes

- Owen's exposition of Hebrews is considered one of the finest commentaries ever written on that epistle
- His works on the Holy Spirit (*Pneumatologia*) represent the most comprehensive treatment of that doctrine in Puritan literature
- Owen's style is dense and scholarly, often requiring careful reading, but rewards the diligent student
- Many of Owen's works were written in response to contemporary theological controversies
- Owen's *Mortification of Sin* and *Communion with God* remain popular devotional works today
