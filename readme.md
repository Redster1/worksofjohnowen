# All of the publicly available works of John Owen

John Owen (1616-1683) was a Puritan theologian and author of numerous theological works.

## Sources

### `/sources/internet-archive/`

Public domain PDFs downloaded from Internet Archive (~1.2GB total):

**Individual Works** (10 works):
- Death of Death in the Death of Christ
- On Temptation and the Mortification of Sin
- Indwelling Sin
- Communion with God
- Glory of Christ
- Doctrine of Justification by Faith
- Pneumatologia (Discourse on the Holy Spirit)
- Forgiveness of Sin (Psalm 130)
- Grace and Duty of Being Spiritually Minded
- Vindiciae Evangelicae

**Complete Works** (19 volumes from Goold edition, 1850-1855)

**Hebrews Exposition** (3 volumes)

See `sources/CATALOG.md` for full details and source links.

**Note:** PDF files are not stored in git due to size (~1.2GB). Run `sources/download_works.sh` to download them from Internet Archive.
